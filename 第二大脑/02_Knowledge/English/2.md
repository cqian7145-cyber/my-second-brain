# Day 20 — Daily English Conversation 🇬🇧

### Topic: Making Decisions（做决定与权衡选择）

**Level:** CET-6 → IELTS 7.0–7.5  
**Time:** 5–8 minutes  
**Today's goal:** Learn to explain your choices, compare alternatives, and express uncertainty naturally.

## 1. Today's Useful Sentence ⭐

**“I'm still weighing up my options, but I'm leaning towards giving it a try.”**

**中文：** 我还在权衡不同的选择，但目前更倾向于尝试一下。

这句话特别适合讨论职业规划、旅行安排、大学生活或个人目标。

**Key expressions:**

**weigh up my options** = 权衡各种选择（英式英语；美式英语也常说 _weigh my options_）。

**lean towards something** = 更倾向于某个选择。

**give it a try** = 尝试一下。

例如：

> “I'm leaning towards taking an internship rather than getting a part-time job.”

我更倾向于参加实习，而不是找一份兼职。

### Grammar Focus

注意 **lean towards + 名词 / 动名词（doing）**。

✅ “I'm leaning towards studying abroad.”

❌ “I'm leaning towards study abroad.”

这里的 _towards_ 是介词，因此后面要接名词或动名词。

## 2. Real-Life Conversation 🎤

**Situation:** You're having lunch with a university friend. You're talking about what to do during the upcoming summer holiday.

**Me:**

“Have you figured out what you're going to do over the summer? I'm torn between applying for an internship and taking a proper break. I know work experience matters, but I also feel like I need some time to recharge.”

**中文：**

你想好暑假要做什么了吗？我还在纠结是申请实习，还是好好休息一下。我知道工作经验很重要，但我也觉得自己需要一些时间恢复精力。

**Useful expressions:**

**figure out** = 想明白、弄清楚。

**be torn between A and B** = 在 A 和 B 之间犹豫不决。

**take a proper break** = 好好休息一段时间。

**recharge** = 恢复精力。

## 3. IELTS 7.5 Speaking Upgrade 🚀

今天学习一个非常实用的表达结构：

**Preference → Reason → Trade-off**

表达倾向 → 解释原因 → 承认代价。

基础表达：

> “I want to do an internship because it is useful.”

更自然：

> “I'm leaning towards doing an internship because I think the hands-on experience would be valuable. That said, I don't want to spend my entire summer working.”

**hands-on experience** = 实践经验。

**That said, ...** = 话虽如此，不过……

这个表达非常重要，因为它能够让你的观点更加平衡。

再看一个例子：

> “On the one hand, I'd love to travel and take a break. On the other hand, I feel like this is a good opportunity to gain some real-world experience.”

**On the one hand… On the other hand…** = 一方面……另一方面……

注意：真实对话中不需要刻意使用所有连接词。选择一两个最适合的即可。

## 4. Today's Speaking Challenge 🎤

**Me:**

“Honestly, if I were you, I'd probably take the internship. You can always travel later, but opportunities to gain practical experience don't come around every day. Then again, I suppose it depends on what matters most to you. What are you leaning towards?”

**中文：**

说真的，如果我是你，我可能会选择实习。以后总有机会旅行，但积累实践经验的机会不是每天都有。不过话说回来，我想这还是取决于你最看重什么。你现在更倾向于哪个选择？

## 5. Today's Speaking Tip 💡

表达不确定性不代表英语水平低。

相反，能够准确表达自己的犹豫和倾向，是自然交流的重要能力。

比较：

**Basic:** “I don't know what to choose.”

**Natural:** “I haven't quite made up my mind yet.”

**More nuanced:** “I'm still weighing up my options, but I'm starting to lean towards one of them.”

其中：

**make up my mind** = 下定决心。

> “I haven't made up my mind yet.”

我还没决定。
