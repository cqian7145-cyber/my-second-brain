# Day 16 — Daily English Conversation 🇬🇧

### Topic: Being Late & Apologizing Naturally（迟到与自然道歉）

**Level:** CET-6 → IELTS 7.0  
**Time:** 5–8 minutes  
**Today's goal:** Learn to explain unexpected delays, apologize naturally, and respond politely.

## 1. Today's Useful Sentence ⭐

**“Sorry to keep you waiting. I got held up on my way here.”**

**中文：** 不好意思让你久等了，我来的路上被事情耽搁了。

**Key expressions:**

**① Sorry to keep you waiting.**

比 _I'm sorry I'm late_ 更贴近日常对话，尤其适用于别人已经等了你一段时间的情况。

**② get held up** = 被耽搁、遇到意外延误。

> “I got held up in traffic.”
> 
> 我遇上堵车，被耽搁了。

> “Something came up at the last minute.”
> 
> 临时出了点事。

**③ on my way here** = 在我来这里的路上。

注意区别：

- **I'm on my way.** = 我正在过去的路上。
- **I'm running late.** = 我快迟到了。
- **I'm stuck in traffic.** = 我被堵在路上了。

## 2. Real-Life Conversation 🎤

**Situation:** We agreed to meet at a café at 3 p.m., but you arrive 20 minutes late.

**Me:**

“Hey, there you are! I was starting to wonder where you'd got to. Everything okay?”

**中文：**

嘿，你终于来了！我刚才还在想你跑哪儿去了呢。一切都还好吗？

**Useful expressions:**

**There you are!** = 你终于出现了！

**I was starting to wonder…** = 我刚刚开始纳闷……

**Where you'd got to** = 你去哪儿了（英式口语）。

这里使用过去完成时，是因为 _where you'd got to_ 表示相对于过去那个“开始纳闷”的时间点，你已经去了哪里。

## 3. IELTS 7.0–7.5 Upgrade 🚀

今天学习如何让道歉听起来更加**自然、得体、有责任感**。

基础表达：

> “Sorry, I'm late because the bus was slow.”

更自然：

> “I'm really sorry I'm late. The bus took forever to arrive, and I ended up getting stuck in traffic.”

**take forever** = 花了特别长的时间（口语中的夸张表达）。

**end up doing something** = 最终做了某事／结果陷入某种情况。

再看一个更成熟的表达：

> “I should've left a bit earlier, to be fair. Thanks for waiting.”

**I should've…** = 我本应该……

**to be fair** = 说句公道话／客观来说。

这个表达体现了你愿意承担责任，而不是一味找借口。

**Grammar focus:**

比较：

**I should leave earlier.**  
我应该早点出发。（现在或将来的建议）

**I should have left earlier.**  
我本应该早点出发。（对过去的反思）

结构：

`should + have + past participle`

例如：

> “I should've checked the weather before leaving.”

我出发前本应该查看天气。

## 4. Your Speaking Challenge 💬

**Me:**

“No worries! I figured something must've come up. I was just about to order something. Do you want to grab a coffee, or would you rather get something to eat first?”

**中文：**

没事！我猜你肯定是临时遇到了什么事。我正准备点东西呢。你想先喝杯咖啡，还是先吃点东西？

**Your task:** Answer naturally in **5–6 English sentences**.

Try to include these expressions:

- **I got held up…**
- **I should've…**
- **I'd rather…**

**Today's challenge:** Apologize, explain what happened, take some responsibility, and respond to your friend's suggestion.

不需要刻意使用复杂句。重点是让对话听起来自然流畅。

### 5. Today's Speaking Tip

雅思口语 7.5 不等于使用大量生僻词。

例如：

❌ _“I sincerely apologize for my delayed arrival due to unforeseen transportation circumstances.”_

虽然语法正确，但在朋友之间显得过于正式。

✅ **“Sorry I'm late! Traffic was a nightmare. I should've left earlier.”**

这才是日常朋友聊天中更自然的表达。

**Phrase of the day: “Sorry to keep you waiting. I got held up on my way here.”**
