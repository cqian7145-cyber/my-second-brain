# Day 18 — Daily English Conversation 🇬🇧

### Topic: Saying No Politely（如何委婉地拒绝别人）

**Level:** CET-6 → IELTS 7.0–7.5  
**Time:** 5–8 minutes  
**Today's goal:** Learn to decline invitations naturally without sounding rude or overly formal.

## 1. Today's Useful Sentence ⭐

**“I'd love to, but I've already got something planned.”**

**中文：** 我很想去，但我已经有别的安排了。

这句话非常适合朋友邀请你吃饭、旅行或参加活动时使用。

**Key expressions:**

**I'd love to** = 我很乐意。

这里即使你最终拒绝邀请，也可以用它表示友好。

**I've already got something planned** = 我已经有安排了。

这是非常自然的口语表达，比 _I have other things to do_ 更委婉。

例如：

**Friend:** “Do you fancy going to the cinema tonight?”

**You:** “I'd love to, but I've already got something planned. How about tomorrow?”

注意，**Do you fancy...?** 是英式英语中常见的邀请方式。

## 2. Real-Life Conversation 🎤

**Situation:** Your friend invites you to a party, but you have an important assignment to finish.

**Me:**

“Hey, a few of us are getting together on Saturday night. We're thinking of grabbing dinner and maybe going to a karaoke place afterwards. You should come! It's been ages since we last hung out.”

**中文：**

嘿，我们几个朋友周六晚上准备聚一聚。打算先吃个饭，然后可能去唱卡拉 OK。你也来吧！我们真的好久没一起玩了。

**Useful expressions:**

**get together** = 聚会、见面。

**grab dinner** = 一起吃顿饭（比较随意）。

**hang out** = 一起玩、消磨时间。

**It's been ages since...** = 自从……以来已经很久了。

## 3. IELTS 7.5 Speaking Upgrade 🚀

今天学习一个重要的交流技巧：

**Appreciation → Reason → Alternative**

表达感谢 → 解释原因 → 提出替代方案。

比较下面两种表达。

**Basic:**

“No, I can't go. I have homework.”

**Natural:**

“Thanks for inviting me! I'd love to come, but I'm a bit tied up with coursework this weekend. Could we catch up sometime next week instead?”

**Key expressions:**

**be tied up with something** = 忙于某事，抽不开身。

> “I'm tied up with a project at the moment.”

我目前正忙着一个项目。

**catch up sometime** = 找个时间叙叙旧。

> “We should catch up sometime next week.”

我们下周应该找个时间聚聚。

### Grammar Focus

注意两个表达：

**I'm busy doing something.**

> “I'm busy preparing for my exams.”

**I'm tied up with something.**

> “I'm tied up with exam preparation.”

前者接动名词，后者通常接名词或名词短语。

## 4. Today's Speaking Challenge 🎤

现在进入真实对话。

**Me:**

“Come on, you can't spend every weekend studying! You've been working so hard lately. Why don't you just take Saturday evening off? I'm sure one night won't make much difference.”

**中文：**

拜托，你总不能每个周末都在学习吧！你最近已经很努力了。为什么不干脆周六晚上休息一下呢？我觉得一个晚上也不会有什么影响。

### 5. Today's Speaking Tip 💡

在真实英语对话中，直接说：

**“I refuse your invitation.”**

虽然语法正确，但听起来非常生硬、正式。

更自然的说法是：

**“I think I'll have to give it a miss this time.”**

意思：**这次我可能得不去了。**

**give something a miss** 是英式英语中常见的口语表达。

例如：

“I'm feeling a bit tired, so I think I'll give the party a miss.”

我有点累，所以这次聚会就不去了。
