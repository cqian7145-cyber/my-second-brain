# Day 19 — Daily English Conversation 🇬🇧

### Topic: Dealing With Disagreements（如何自然地表达不同意见）

**Level:** CET-6 → IELTS 7.0–7.5  
**Time:** 5–8 minutes  
**Today's goal:** Learn to disagree politely while keeping the conversation friendly.

## 1. Today's Useful Sentence ⭐

**“I see where you're coming from, but I don't entirely agree.”**

**中文：** 我理解你的想法，但我不完全赞同。

这句话在日常英语交流中非常实用，尤其适合朋友讨论不同观点时使用。

**Key expressions:**

**see where someone is coming from** = 理解某人的观点或立场。

> “I can see where you're coming from.”

我能理解你为什么这么想。

**don't entirely agree** = 不完全赞同。

相比直接说 _I disagree_，这种表达更加委婉。

**Natural alternatives:**

- **“You've got a point.”** 你说得有道理。
- **“I'm not so sure about that.”** 我对此不太确定。
- **“That's one way of looking at it.”** 这也是一种看法。

注意：最后一句有时也可能带有委婉质疑的意味，具体取决于语气。

## 2. Real-Life Conversation 🎤

**Situation:** You're having coffee with a university friend. The conversation turns to whether university students should work part-time.

**Me:**

“Honestly, I think university students should focus entirely on their studies. Getting a part-time job just takes up valuable time, and most students already have enough on their plates. Don't you think?”

**中文：**

说真的，我觉得大学生应该把全部精力放在学习上。兼职只会占用宝贵的时间，而且大多数学生本来就已经够忙了。你不觉得吗？

**Useful expressions:**

**focus entirely on something** = 全身心投入某事。

**take up time** = 占用时间。

**have enough on one's plate** = 已经有很多事情要处理。

## 3. IELTS 7.5 Speaking Upgrade 🚀

今天学习一个重要技巧：

**Acknowledge → Disagree → Explain → Give an Example**

理解对方 → 表达不同意见 → 解释原因 → 举例说明。

基础表达：

> “I disagree. Part-time jobs are useful.”

更自然：

> “I see where you're coming from, but I think part-time jobs can actually be beneficial, provided students manage their time properly.”

**beneficial** = 有益的。

**provided (that)** = 只要、倘若。

进一步表达：

> “For instance, working part-time can help students become more independent and develop practical skills that they wouldn't necessarily pick up in a classroom.”

**pick up a skill** = 在实践中逐渐学会某项技能。

**wouldn't necessarily** = 不一定会。

### Grammar Focus

比较：

**Students can learn skills at work.**

学生可以在工作中学习技能。

**Students can develop skills that they wouldn't necessarily acquire through formal education.**

学生可以培养一些不一定能通过正规教育获得的技能。

第二句使用了**定语从句**，表达更精确。

但在真实口语中，_pick up_ 通常比 _acquire_ 更自然。

## 4. Today's Speaking Challenge 🎤

**Me:**

“Okay, I get your point, but wouldn't having a part-time job make it harder to keep up with university coursework? I mean, if you're already busy with classes and personal projects, isn't adding a job just going to make things more stressful?”

**中文：**

好吧，我理解你的观点。但兼职难道不会让你更难跟上大学课程吗？我的意思是，如果你已经忙于上课和个人项目，再加上一份工作，难道不会让压力更大吗？

尝试自然使用：

- **“That's a fair point, but…”**（你说得有道理，不过……）
- **“I think it depends on…”**（我觉得这取决于……）
- **“As long as…”**（只要……）

**Difficulty upgrade:** 不要完全否定对方。先承认兼职可能带来的问题，再解释为什么你仍然支持或反对兼职。

你也可以选择完全支持我的观点，重点是把理由解释清楚。

## 5. Today's Speaking Tip 💡

在 IELTS Speaking 中，表达观点不需要绝对化。

例如：

❌ “Part-time jobs are always good for students.”

✅ “Part-time jobs can be beneficial, depending on the student's circumstances.”

**depending on the circumstances** = 视具体情况而定。

这个表达能让你的观点更加客观、灵活。

不过，IELTS 7.5 并不要求每句话都复杂。准确性、连贯性和自然表达同样重要。
