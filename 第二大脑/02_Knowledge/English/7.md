# Day 15 — Daily English Conversation 🇬🇧

### Topic: Dealing With Unexpected Changes（应对突发变化）

**Level:** CET-6 → IELTS 7.0  
**Time:** 5–8 minutes  
**Today's goal:** Learn to respond naturally when plans don't go as expected.

## 1. Today's Useful Sentence ⭐

**“Things didn't quite go as planned, but we made the most of it.”**

**中文：** 事情没有完全按照计划发展，但我们还是尽量玩得开心了。

**Key expressions:**

**① didn't quite go as planned** = 没有完全按计划进行。

这里的 _quite_ 让语气更加委婉。

Compare:

- Basic: _Our plan failed._
- Natural: _Things didn't go as planned._
- More nuanced: _Things didn't quite go as planned._

**② make the most of something** = 充分利用某种情况，尽可能从中获得好处。

> “It started raining halfway through our trip, but we made the most of it.”

旅行到一半开始下雨了，但我们还是尽量玩得开心。

注意：**make the most of** 不等于 **make the best of**。

两者都可以表示充分利用，但 _make the best of_ 更常用于不理想的情况。

## 2. Real-Life Conversation 🎤

**Situation:** We're visiting West Lake, but it suddenly starts raining.

**Me:**

“Oh no, it's absolutely pouring! I thought the weather was supposed to be nice today. We were going to walk around the lake, but I guess that's off the table now. What should we do?”

**中文：**

天哪，雨下得也太大了！我还以为今天天气会很好呢。我们本来打算沿着湖边散步，但现在看来是不可能了。我们怎么办？

**Useful conversational expressions:**

**It's pouring!** = 正在下倾盆大雨！

**be supposed to** = 按理说应该……

**be off the table** = 不再是可考虑的选项。

## 3. IELTS 7.0–7.5 Upgrade 🚀

今天练习一个重要能力：**Spontaneous Problem-Solving（即兴解决问题）**。

基础表达：

> “It's raining. We can go to a café.”

更自然的表达：

> “Since the weather's taken a turn for the worse, why don't we find somewhere cozy to wait out the rain?”

**take a turn for the worse** = 情况突然变糟。

**somewhere cozy** = 一个舒适惬意的地方。

**wait out the rain** = 等雨停。

再升级一点：

> “It's a shame we can't stick to our original plan, but we could always check out a nearby café instead.”

**It's a shame…** = 真可惜……

**stick to our original plan** = 坚持原来的计划。

**We could always…** = 我们也可以……（提出备选方案）。

这些表达能够帮助你在 IELTS Speaking Part 2 和 Part 3 中更灵活地描述意外情况和解决办法。

## 4. Your Speaking Challenge 💬

**Me:**

“Well, it doesn't look like the rain is going to stop anytime soon. I'm a bit disappointed because I was really looking forward to walking around the lake. Do you think we should wait it out or change our plans altogether?”

**中文：**

看来这雨一时半会儿停不了。我有点失望，因为我本来真的很期待沿着湖边散步。你觉得我们应该等雨停，还是干脆改变计划？

**Your task:** Answer naturally in **5–6 English sentences**.

Try to use today's expressions:

- **It's a shame that…**
- **We could always…**
- **make the most of it**

**Today's challenge:** Acknowledge your friend's disappointment, suggest an alternative, and explain why it's a good idea.
