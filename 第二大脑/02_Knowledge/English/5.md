# Day 17 — Daily English Conversation 🇬🇧

### Topic: Making Small Talk（如何自然地闲聊）

**Level:** CET-6 → IELTS 7.0  
**Time:** 5–8 minutes  
**Today's goal:** Learn to keep a conversation going naturally instead of giving short, disconnected answers.

## 1. Today's Useful Sentence ⭐

**“I've been meaning to ask you, how's everything going with your studies?”**

**中文：** 我一直想问你，最近学习怎么样？

**Key expression: have been meaning to do something**

表示“早就想做某事，但一直没有找到合适的机会”。

例如：

“I've been meaning to try that new restaurant.”

我早就想试试那家新餐厅了。

“I've been meaning to get back into exercising.”

我一直想重新开始锻炼。

**Grammar focus:**

`have/has + been + meaning + to + 动词原形`

注意：

- **I mean to ask you.** 表示我打算问你，但在这个情境中不够自然。
- **I've been meaning to ask you.** 强调这个想法已经持续了一段时间。

这是日常聊天中非常实用的表达。

## 2. Real-Life Conversation 🎤

**Situation:** You run into a classmate at university. You haven't spoken properly in a few weeks.

**Me:**

“Hey! It's been a while! I've been meaning to catch up with you. How's university treating you these days? Still buried in assignments?”

**中文：**

嘿！好久没好好聊聊了！我一直想找你叙叙旧。最近大学生活怎么样？还在被作业压得喘不过气吗？

**Useful expressions:**

**catch up with someone** = 和某人叙旧、聊聊近况。

**How's university treating you?** = 最近大学生活过得怎么样？

**be buried in something** = 忙于某事，事情多得做不完。

例如：

“I've been buried in coursework lately.”

最近我一直忙着完成课程作业。

## 3. IELTS 7.0–7.5 Upgrade 🚀

今天学习一个重要的口语技巧：

**Answer + Detail + Return Question**

回答 → 补充细节 → 反问对方。

普通回答：

“I'm fine. University is busy.”

更自然的回答：

“Pretty good, actually! I've been quite busy with coursework lately, but I'm enjoying it more than I expected. What about you? How have things been on your end?”

**on your end** = 你那边、你那里的情况。

这个表达很适合朋友聊天。

**Why does this matter?**

在真实对话中，流利不只是能够连续说很多句英语，还包括能够自然地回应别人、补充信息，并推动对话继续。

不过，在 IELTS Speaking 考试中，不需要反问考官。这个技巧主要用于日常交流。

## 4. Today's Speaking Challenge 🎤

**Me:**

“By the way, I've been meaning to ask you something. You always seem to have so many things going on. How do you manage to balance your studies, personal projects, and free time? Do you ever feel overwhelmed?”

**中文：**

对了，我一直想问你一个问题。感觉你总是有很多事情要忙。你是怎么平衡学习、个人项目和空闲时间的？你会不会有时候觉得压力特别大？

### Your task

请用英语回答 **5–7 句话**，尽量像和朋友聊天，而不是背诵作文。

今天尝试使用三个表达：

1. **To be fair, ...**（说实话／客观来说……）
2. **I've been trying to...**（我最近一直在努力……）
3. **Every now and then, ...**（偶尔、有时候……）

**Difficulty upgrade:** 今天尝试在回答中加入一个具体例子，而不只是表达观点。
