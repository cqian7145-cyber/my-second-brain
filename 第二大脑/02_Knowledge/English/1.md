# 🇬🇧 Day 21 — Daily English Conversation

### Topic: Talking About Your Future（谈论未来规划）

**Level:** CET-6 → IELTS 7.0–7.5  
**Time:** 5–8 minutes  
**Today's goal:** Learn to talk naturally about your ambitions, career plans, and uncertainties.

## 1. Today's Useful Sentence ⭐

**“I haven't got everything figured out yet, but I have a pretty clear idea of where I want to be in a few years.”**

**中文：** 虽然我还没有把所有事情都规划清楚，但我对未来几年想达到的目标已经有了比较明确的想法。

这是一个非常自然的表达，适合讨论大学生活、职业规划和人生目标。

**Key expressions:**

**① have something figured out** = 已经想明白、弄清楚某件事。

> “I still haven't figured out what I want to do after graduation.”

我还没想清楚毕业后想做什么。

**② have a clear idea of…** = 对……有明确的想法。

> “I have a clear idea of what kind of career I want to pursue.”

我很清楚自己想从事什么样的职业。

**③ where I want to be** = 我希望自己未来达到的状态。

这里的 _where_ 不一定指地理位置，也可以表示人生阶段、职业成就或个人发展状态。

## 2. Real-Life Conversation 🎤

**Situation:** You're having dinner with a friend who is about to graduate from university.

**Me:**

“It's crazy how quickly time flies, isn't it? I feel like we only started university yesterday. Have you given much thought to what you want to do after graduation?”

**中文：**

时间过得真快，是吧？感觉我们昨天才刚上大学。你有没有认真考虑过毕业后想做什么？

**Useful expressions:**

**Time flies.** = 时间过得飞快。

**give something some thought** = 认真考虑某件事。

**after graduation** = 毕业后。

例如：

> “I've been giving my career plans a lot of thought lately.”

最近我一直在认真考虑自己的职业规划。

## 3. IELTS 7.5 Speaking Upgrade 🚀

今天练习如何表达**长期目标与现实之间的差距**。

基础表达：

> “I want to start my own business, but it's difficult.”

更自然：

> “Ideally, I'd like to start my own business someday, but I know I'll need to gain some practical experience first.”

**Ideally, ...** = 理想情况下……

**gain practical experience** = 积累实践经验。

进一步升级：

> “In the long run, I'd love to work for myself. That said, I'm aware that building a successful business takes more than just having a good idea.”

**in the long run** = 从长远来看。

**work for myself** = 自己创业、为自己工作。

**I'm aware that…** = 我清楚地知道……

**takes more than just…** = 不仅仅需要……

### Grammar Focus: Future Possibilities

比较以下三种表达：

|Expression|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**I'm going to start a business.**|我打算创业，已有较明确的计划。|
|**I'd like to start a business.**|我希望创业，表达愿望。|
|**I might start a business.**|我可能会创业，表达不确定性。|

**IELTS Tip:** 不要为了显得高级而使用过多复杂句。能够准确区分计划、愿望和可能性，比堆砌高级词汇更重要。

## 4. Today's Speaking Challenge 💬

“That's interesting! Personally, I think having a clear direction matters more than having every little detail planned out. But I'm curious—do you see yourself working for a company after graduation, or would you rather build something of your own?”

挺有意思的！我个人觉得，有一个明确的方向比把每个细节都规划好更重要。不过我很好奇，你觉得自己毕业后会去公司工作，还是更想自己创业？

## 5. Today's Speaking Tip 💡

英语口语中，表达雄心不需要过度正式。

❌ “My ultimate objective is to establish an entrepreneurial enterprise.”

虽然能够理解，但听起来生硬、不自然。

✅ **“My long-term goal is to start my own business.”**

✅ **“Eventually, I'd love to build something of my own.”**

第二句尤其适合朋友之间的自然交流。

**build something of my own** 不仅可以指创业，还可以指创造自己的产品、项目或事业。
